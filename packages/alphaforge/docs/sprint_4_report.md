## What the evidence actually shows

**Execution perturbation moves the result materially.** Against an unperturbed
net return of −19.7%, the 5th-percentile path under stale-signal timing error
reaches −31.9%. Cost inflation degrades every single replicate.

**Path ordering is the largest drawdown risk.** Reordering the *same* realized
returns — which leaves compounded return mathematically invariant, since
multiplication commutes — deepens worst drawdown from −35.1% to −52.3%. A result
can be identical in total and far worse in experience.

**No path reached insolvency**, the one criterion the candidate satisfies.

## Defects found and fixed during the sprint

Recorded because each produced a plausible-looking number that was wrong:

- **A timing perturbation that could only help.** Blending a fraction of each bar
  into the next is a smoothing operator; smoothing a fixed-sum path always lowers
  variance drag, so every replicate improved and the arm reported a 0.000
  frequency of underperforming. Replaced with stale-signal substitution.
- **A boundary leak** in that same arm silently deleted the final bar's carried
  return from every replicate.
- **A vacuous metric.** `trade_order` was reporting a net-return distribution
  that was floating-point noise. The arm was kept for its genuine drawdown
  effect, the invariance pinned by test, and the arm excluded by name from the
  two-sidedness guard rather than passing it by luck.
- **A stale figure caption** asserting a finding the corrected data no longer
  supported. Captions are now computed from the data.

Earlier in the sprint: a min-variance solve returning all zeros, a turnover
penalty that increased turnover, a radial L1 rescaling that was not a projection,
a reconciliation tolerance derived from the residual it was checking, and a
no-effect axis that manufactured a sensitivity cliff from its own noise.

## Limitations and what Sprint 4 does not establish

- **No strategy is qualified.** No paper or live trading is authorized, no broker
  is contacted, and no capital is at risk.
- **Synthetic development data only.** Not a backtest on licensed point-in-time
  data; the universe is not survivorship-free.
- **Monte Carlo frequencies are model frequencies**, not probabilities of loss in
  the market. The perturbation family is an assumption.
- **Perturbation arms are stressed independently.** Interaction effects — a
  missed trade *and* a liquidity shock in the same bar — are not modelled.
- **A frozen rubric prevents moving the bar; it does not make the bar correct.**

## What remains before paper trading is possible

1. Licensed point-in-time data with corporate actions, delistings, and
   survivorship-free universe membership (Signalattice).
2. A complete trial ledger so multiple-testing correction has a family to correct
   over.
3. Capacity and concentration measurement on a real candidate.
4. Broker connectivity requirements and selection (#99, SF-S5-MR2), then the
   broker contract (#45) and implementation (#88).
